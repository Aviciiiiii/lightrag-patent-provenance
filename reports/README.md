# reports/ — dot's original reports

- [`original/`](original/): copies of the Chinese HTML reports generated by dot, plus `.md` conversions for reading on the web. These are the evidence and are kept unchanged apart from the two replacements listed below. **The originals are preserved unchanged in the author's local evidence package.**
- [`translated/`](translated/): English translations of the `.md` files, for readability. **The Chinese originals prevail.** They were translated with AI assistance (Claude Code), segment by segment (each paragraph or table cell containing Chinese). Numbers, identifiers, code, and quotations from patents and model output (already English in the originals) are copied unchanged; only the Chinese prose is translated. Every translated segment was checked automatically to contain exactly the same numbers as its original, and no Chinese text is left in the translations. Each translation starts with a link to its original and the statement that the Chinese original prevails.

## What the copies change relative to the originals

Only two kinds of replacement; every other byte is unchanged:

1. **Local paths replaced by placeholders**: **1** occurrence in the 5 reports (per report below). Placeholders: [`../docs/NAMING.md`](../docs/NAMING.md).
2. **A personal application paragraph unrelated to the experiments removed**: dot appended an English “research interest paragraph” to several reports; the copies remove that section and leave a “(removed)” marker in its place, 8 occurrences in total (including the collapsed history snapshots at the end of report 03).

The `.md` files are generated from these HTML copies by [`../tools/html_to_md.py`](../tools/html_to_md.py): body text and tables are complete; history snapshots that are collapsed or embedded in the HTML (except the round-two report at the end of report 02, which is part of the body) are not converted; see the HTML for them.
